Cookie Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Homify does not use advertising or third-party analytics cookies. We only use storage that is needed to run the site, plus services that make the map and sign-in work. This policy explains each one and how you can control it.

1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We also use your browser's local storage and session storage, which work in a similar way, to keep you signed in and remember your preferences.

2. Storage we use

  • Sign-in session - we store a secure token in your browser's session storage so you stay logged in while you use Homify. This is strictly necessary.
  • Your consent choice - we remember whether you accepted or declined this notice so we do not ask again.
  • Saved items and preferences - for example, properties you favour and your last search, stored in your browser.

These are stored on your device and are used to operate and secure the site. They are not used to track you across other websites.

3. Third-party services that may set cookies

When you use Homify, these providers may receive limited technical data (such as your IP address) and may set their own cookies:

  • Content delivery networks (cdnjs and unpkg) - deliver fonts, icons and the map software.
  • OpenStreetMap - provides the map tiles on listing pages.
  • Google - powers the optional "Sign in with Google" button and opens Google Maps when you tap "Open in Maps".

These providers' own privacy and cookie policies apply to data they process. We do not control those cookies, but we do not add any advertising or profiling cookies of our own.

4. Your choices

You can accept or decline this notice using the buttons in the banner. You can also clear or block cookies at any time in your browser settings. Note that blocking strictly necessary storage may stop sign-in and some features from working.

Because Homify uses only necessary storage and no advertising trackers, the site works fully whether you accept or decline the notice.

5. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. See our Privacy Policy for more on how we handle data.

This page is provided for general information and does not constitute legal advice.
