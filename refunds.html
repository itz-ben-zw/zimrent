Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Homify is currently free to use. We do not charge listing fees, commissions or subscriptions, and we do not collect rent or deposits. Because we do not take payments today, there is generally nothing to refund.

1. Rent and deposits are not paid to Homify

Any rent, deposit or other payment is agreed and paid directly between the tenant and the landlord. Homify is not a party to that arrangement and cannot issue refunds for rent or deposits. If you have a dispute, you must take it up with the other party.

2. If we introduce paid features in future

If Homify adds optional paid features (for example, a featured or boosted listing), we will give you clear notice and show the price before you pay. For any such paid feature:

  • You can cancel and request a refund within 7 days of purchase if the paid feature has not yet been delivered.
  • If we fail to deliver a paid feature, or it is materially not as described, you are entitled to a refund of the amount paid.
  • Refunds are returned to the original payment method within a reasonable time.

These rules follow the consumer protections in Zimbabwe's Consumer Protection Act 2019. Nothing here removes rights you have under the law.

3. How to request a refund

Email homify.online@gmail.com with your account email and details of the payment. We will respond within a reasonable time.

This page is provided for general information and does not constitute legal advice. Homify recommends review by a qualified Zimbabwean attorney before launch.
