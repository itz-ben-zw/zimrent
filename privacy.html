4. Marketing

We do not send marketing email unless you separately opt in. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us, and we will stop.

5. How we share information

When you list a property or contact a landlord/tenant, the contact details you provide are shown to the other party so you can reach each other. Beyond that, we do not sell your personal information. We use trusted service providers to operate the site:

  • Cloud hosting and a managed PostgreSQL database (data is stored on servers located outside Zimbabwe, with safeguards in place).
  • Content delivery networks (cdnjs, unpkg) that deliver fonts, icons and the map library.
  • OpenStreetMap for map tiles and Google for the "Sign in with Google" option.

These providers may process limited technical data (such as your IP address) to deliver their services. See our Cookie Policy for details.

6. How long we keep information

We keep your account information while your account is open. Listing and message data is kept until you delete it or close your account. Security logs are kept only as long as needed for security purposes. When you delete your account we remove or anonymise your personal information, except where we must keep certain records to comply with the law.

  • Withdraw consent for marketing at any time.
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to any legal retention requirements.

To exercise any right, email homify.online@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and may ask you to verify your identity first.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including hashed passwords and secured connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the relevant authority if a breach occurs as required by law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
