Stay Safe

A few tips before you commit

  • Always view a property in person before paying any deposit.
  • Meet the landlord during the day, ideally with a friend or family member.
  • Homify never handles payments — pay the landlord directly and keep your receipts.
  • If something feels wrong, don't proceed and let us know.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Is Homify free to use?

Yes. Students can search listings and contact landlords for free.

Does Homify own the properties?

No. Homify is a listing platform. Landlords own and manage the properties they post.

How do I contact a landlord?

Open a listing and use the phone number, WhatsApp link or email shown on the page.

Are the listings verified?

We don't verify listings ourselves. Always view a place in person before paying.

Can I list my own room?

Yes. Create an account, choose to list a property, and add your room with photos and location.

Which areas do you cover?

Suburbs near NUST in Bulawayo, including Selbourne Park, Riverside and Matsheumhlope.
